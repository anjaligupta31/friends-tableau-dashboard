# Friends Sitcom Analytics Dashboard | Tableau

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Tableau dashboard designed to analyze episode-level performance of the sitcom Friends across its 10 seasons.

The dashboard provides a visual exploration of episode ratings, audience votes, episode duration, season-wise episode counts, and director contributions.

The objective of the project is to transform episode-level data into an interactive analytical experience that makes it easy to explore trends in audience ratings and engagement across seasons and episodes.

## Objectives

- Analyze episode ratings across all 10 seasons
- Compare audience engagement using total votes
- Identify rating patterns across episodes and seasons
- Compare the number of episodes released in each season
- Explore the contribution of different directors
- Provide detailed information for individual episodes
- Build an interactive and visually engaging Tableau dashboard

## Key Analysis Areas

- Episode ratings by season
- Total votes by episode and season
- Number of episodes by season
- Director-level analysis
- Episode duration
- Episode title and description
- Individual episode performance

## Key Insights

### 1. The series spans 10 seasons

The dashboard provides episode-level analysis across all 10 seasons of Friends, allowing ratings and audience engagement to be compared throughout the show's run.

### 2. Most seasons contain approximately 24–25 episodes

Seasons 1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 8 and 9 contain 24 episodes, while Seasons 3 and 6 contain 25 episodes. Season 10 contains 18 episodes.

This provides useful context when comparing total audience votes across seasons.

### 3. Episode ratings generally remain strong across seasons

The episode-level rating distribution shows that ratings are concentrated around the 8+ range across most seasons, with several episodes reaching ratings above 9.

This indicates consistently strong audience reception throughout the series.

### 4. Audience engagement varies considerably by episode

The total-votes visualization shows substantial differences in audience participation across episodes, with some episodes attracting close to 40K votes.

This provides a useful way to distinguish highly engaged episodes from those with comparatively lower audience participation.

### 5. Season 10 has fewer episodes

Season 10 contains 18 episodes compared with approximately 24–25 episodes in most earlier seasons.

Therefore, direct comparisons of total votes between seasons should account for the difference in episode count.

### 6. Episode-level analysis provides additional context

The dashboard allows an individual episode to be examined through its title, season, year, duration, rating, vote count and description.

For example, the selected episode "The One Where Eddie Moves In" is from Season 2, Episode 17, aired in 1996, has a duration of 22 minutes, a rating of 8.1 and 3,407 votes.

### 7. Director contribution can be explored across the series

The dashboard includes directors such as Kevin Bright, Gary Halvorson, Michael Lembeck, James Burrows and others, enabling exploration of directing contributions across the show's episodes.


## Analytical Takeaways

- Episode ratings can be used as a measure of audience satisfaction and content performance.
- Total votes provide an additional measure of audience engagement beyond rating alone.
- Season-level comparisons should consider differences in the number of episodes.
- Episode-level analysis enables identification of particularly successful or highly engaged episodes.
- Director-level analysis can be used to explore whether certain directors are associated with consistently strong audience reception.
- Combining ratings and vote counts provides a more complete view of episode performance than relying on ratings alone.
